From my heart, honestly

I miss you,
but I'll let you go.

There are days when I miss you more than I can explain. I miss everything about you and the memories we shared. Sometimes, I wish things could have been different. I wish the happy moments could stay a little longer, and I wish the silence didn't feel so heavy. But I know that missing someone doesn't mean I can ask them to stay. And loving someone doesn't give me the right to cross their boundaries. I understand that things are different now. I respect your space, and I won't force a conversation or ask you for a reply. A part of me will always be grateful for the moments that made me smile. Even if our story doesn't continue, those memories were real to me. I'm learning that letting go doesn't mean forgetting. It means accepting what I cannot change, taking care of my own heart, and allowing both of us to move forward. I hope life brings you peace, happiness, and everything good that you deserve. I miss you. I really do. But this time, I'll let my feelings remain words on a page, and I'll let you have your space.
