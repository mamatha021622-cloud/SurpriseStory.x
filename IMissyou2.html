I miss you,

There are days when I miss you more than I can explain. I miss everything about you and the memories we shared. Sometimes I wish things could have been different. I wish the happy moments could have lasted longer, and I wish the silence didn't feel so heavy. I still remember the little things. The conversations, the laughter, and the moments that made me smile. Those memories still mean something to me, even though things are different now. But I'm slowly understanding something: missing you doesn't mean I can ask you to stay. And caring about you doesn't give me the right to cross your boundaries. I respect your space. I won't force a conversation or ask you for a reply. You don't owe me an explanation or a promise. A part of me wishes our story had turned out differently. But I cannot change what has happened, and I cannot hold on to someone who doesn't want to talk to me. So I'll keep the good memories with gratitude. I'll allow myself to miss you without turning that feeling into a reason to reach out. I hope life is kind to you. I hope you find happiness, peace, and all the beautiful things you deserve. I miss you. I really do. But this time, I'll let my feelings remain words on a page. I'll respect your silence, take care of my own heart, and slowly learn to move forward. Not because the memories meant nothing, but because they meant something. And now, it's time to let go.
